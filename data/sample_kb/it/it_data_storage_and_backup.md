# NovaRetail IT: Data Storage, Backup and Recovery

Owner: IT Infrastructure | Last reviewed: 2026-01-30

## OneDrive
Every employee has 1 TB of OneDrive storage. The Desktop, Documents, and Pictures folders on company laptops are automatically redirected to OneDrive, so they are backed up continuously while the laptop is online. Files saved elsewhere on the local disk (for example C:\Temp) are not backed up.

## Recovering Deleted Files
Deleted OneDrive and SharePoint files stay in the recycle bin for 93 days and can be restored by the user. Previous versions of a file can be restored from the file's Version History. After 93 days, recovery requires a Service Desk ticket and is not guaranteed.

## USB Storage
USB storage devices (flash drives, external disks) are blocked by default on company laptops. An exception requires Information Security approval through a Service Desk request and is valid for 30 days. Approved devices must be company-issued encrypted drives.

## Shared Team Data
Team documents must be stored in the team's SharePoint site or Teams channel, not in an individual's OneDrive, so that access continues when people change roles or leave.

## Retention After Departure
When an employee leaves, their OneDrive is retained for 90 days and access can be delegated to their manager on request. After 90 days it is permanently deleted.
