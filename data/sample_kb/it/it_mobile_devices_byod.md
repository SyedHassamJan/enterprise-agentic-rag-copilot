# NovaRetail IT: Mobile Devices and Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Owner: IT Service Desk | Last reviewed: 2026-02-05

## Personal Phones (BYOD)
Employees may access company email and Teams on a personal phone after installing the Intune Company Portal app and enrolling the device. Minimum supported versions are iOS 17 and Android 13. Jailbroken or rooted devices are blocked automatically.

## What IT Can and Cannot See
On an enrolled personal phone, IT can see the device model, OS version, and the company apps. IT cannot see personal photos, messages, browsing history, or personal apps. If the phone is lost or the employee leaves, IT performs a selective wipe that removes only company apps and data.

## Company Phones
Company-paid phones are provided only to on-call staff, store managers, and roles approved by a director. Company phones are fully managed and may be completely wiped if lost.

## Mobile Data Costs
NovaRetail does not reimburse personal mobile data plans for BYOD use. Employees in roles that require heavy mobile use should request a company phone through their manager.

## Lost Phones
Report a lost phone that has company apps installed to the IT Service Desk the same day so the company data can be removed.
