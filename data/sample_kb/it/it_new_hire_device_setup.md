# NovaRetail IT: New Hire Device and Account Setup

Owner: IT Service Desk | Last reviewed: 2026-02-10

## Account Provisioning
Hiring managers must submit the IT access request at least 10 business days before the employee's start date. IT creates the company email account, identity portal login, and default group memberships after HR confirms the start date. Requests submitted with less notice are processed on a best-effort basis and access may not be ready on day one.

## Standard Laptop
Every new office or hybrid employee receives a standard 14-inch NovaBook laptop with 16 GB of memory. Engineering and data roles receive the 16-inch NovaBook Pro with 32 GB of memory. Laptops for remote hires are shipped so that they arrive 3 business days before the start date. Employees must not install a personal operating system or remove the device management profile.

## First Login Checklist
1. Connect to a trusted network and sign in with the temporary password sent to your personal email.
2. Change the temporary password immediately. Passwords must be at least 14 characters.
3. Enroll the company authenticator app within 24 hours of first login. Accounts without MFA enrollment are locked after 24 hours.
4. Install the NovaSecure VPN client and any role-specific tools from the Software Portal.
5. Confirm that OneDrive sync is active for Desktop and Documents.

## Peripherals
A standard peripheral kit (keyboard, mouse, headset) is included with every laptop. Additional monitors and docking stations can be ordered through the Hardware Portal with manager approval. IT does not support or reimburse personally purchased hardware.
