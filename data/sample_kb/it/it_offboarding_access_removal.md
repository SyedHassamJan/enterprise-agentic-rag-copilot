# NovaRetail IT: Offboarding and Access Removal

Owner: IT Service Desk | Last reviewed: 2026-02-18

## Account Deactivation
All accounts of a departing employee (email, VPN, identity portal, and business applications) are disabled at 5:00 PM local time on the final working day confirmed by HR. For involuntary terminations, access is disabled immediately when HR submits the request.

## Equipment Return
Laptops, company phones, security keys, and badges must be returned within 5 business days after the final working day. Remote employees receive a prepaid return kit by courier. If equipment is not returned, IT notifies the manager and HR, and the asset is reported as unrecovered.

## Mailbox and Files
The mailbox is converted to a shared mailbox and retained for 90 days; the manager can request access. OneDrive data follows the 90-day retention rule in the Data Storage policy. Auto-replies can be configured on request.

## Manager Checklist
Before the last day, the manager should transfer ownership of shared documents, Teams channels, and SharePoint sites, and confirm which licensed software can be reclaimed.
