# NovaRetail IT: Acceptable Use Policy

Owner: Information Security | Last reviewed: 2026-03-01

## Personal Use
Limited personal use of company devices is permitted if it does not interfere with work, consume significant resources, or violate any policy. Company devices must never be used for cryptocurrency mining, illegal downloads, gambling, or running personal businesses.

## Cloud Storage and File Sharing
Company data must be stored only in approved locations: OneDrive, SharePoint, and Teams. Personal cloud storage services such as Dropbox, personal Google Drive, or personal iCloud must not be used for company data. External sharing links from OneDrive expire after 30 days by default.

## Generative AI Tools
NovaAssist, the company's internal AI assistant, is approved for work use including confidential data. Public AI chatbots may be used only for non-confidential tasks. Never paste customer data, employee personal data, source code, credentials, or financial results into public AI tools. Violations are treated as a data-handling incident.

## Monitoring
Company devices, email, and network traffic may be monitored for security and compliance purposes in accordance with applicable law. Employees should have no expectation of privacy for content stored on company systems.

## Consequences
Violations of this policy may result in loss of access and disciplinary action up to and including termination, coordinated with HR.
