# NovaRetail IT: Privileged Access Runbook (RESTRICTED)

Owner: IT Infrastructure | Last reviewed: 2026-03-02 | Status: CURRENT | Access: it_staff

## Break-Glass Admin Accounts
Break-glass administrator credentials are stored in NovaVault in the "Emergency Access" folder. Checking out a break-glass credential requires approval from 2 IT administrators. Every use must be recorded in a P1 ticket within 1 hour.

## Credential Rotation
Break-glass credentials rotate automatically after each checkout and at least every 90 days. Service account secrets are rotated every 180 days.

## Admin Rights Requests
Standing administrator rights are granted only to the infrastructure team. All other admin access is just-in-time, expires after 8 hours, and is requested through the Privileged Access portal.
