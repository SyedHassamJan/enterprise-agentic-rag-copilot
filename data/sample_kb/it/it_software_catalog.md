# NovaRetail IT: Software Catalog and Licensing

Owner: IT Asset Management | Last reviewed: 2026-03-12

## Standard Software (no approval needed)
Microsoft 365 (Outlook, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams, OneDrive), NovaSecure VPN, the company authenticator app, Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, 7-Zip, and NovaAssist are available to every employee from the Software Portal.

## Licensed Software (manager approval required)
Adobe Acrobat Pro, Visio, Project, Tableau, and JetBrains IDEs require manager approval. The license cost is charged to the requesting department's budget. Approved requests are fulfilled within 3 business days.

## Not Approved
Slack, WhatsApp Desktop, personal VPN apps, and remote-access tools such as TeamViewer or AnyDesk are not approved. Use Microsoft Teams for chat and IT-provided remote support tools only.

## Requesting New Software
Software that is not in the catalog requires a Software Portal request with a business justification. IT Security reviews new software within 10 business days. Administrator rights are never granted solely to install software.

## License Reclamation
Paid licenses that are unused for 60 days are automatically reclaimed and returned to the pool. Users are notified 7 days before reclamation.
