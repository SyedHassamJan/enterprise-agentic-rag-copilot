# NovaRetail IT: Remote Work Security Standard

Owner: Information Security | Last reviewed: 2026-01-15

## Home Network
Home Wi-Fi used for company work must use WPA2 or WPA3 encryption and must not use the router's default admin password. The NovaSecure VPN must be connected before accessing any internal system, including the HR portal, finance systems, and internal file shares.

## Public Networks
Working from public Wi-Fi (cafes, airports, hotels) is allowed only while the VPN is connected. Do not use public or shared computers to access company systems under any circumstances. Mobile hotspot from a personal phone is preferred over public Wi-Fi.

## Physical Security
Lock your screen whenever you leave the laptop (Windows key + L). Do not leave company devices in a parked car. Confidential or restricted documents must not be printed on home printers; use office printers with secure release instead.

## Video Calls
Use Microsoft Teams for internal and customer calls. Blur or replace your background when confidential material, whiteboards, or other people are visible. Do not record calls without informing all participants.

## Equipment at Home
Company laptops, monitors, and docking stations used at home remain company property and are tracked by asset tag. Home office equipment must be ordered through the IT Hardware Portal; personally purchased IT equipment cannot be connected to the corporate network except standard USB keyboards, mice, and headsets.
