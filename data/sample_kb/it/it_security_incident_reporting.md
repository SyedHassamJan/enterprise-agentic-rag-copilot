# NovaRetail IT: Security Incident Reporting

Owner: Security Operations Center (SOC) | Last reviewed: 2026-02-20

## Phishing Emails
If you receive a suspicious email, use the "Report Phish" button in Outlook. Do not forward the email to colleagues, do not click links, and do not open attachments. Reported messages are analysed by the SOC and removed from all mailboxes if confirmed malicious.

## If You Clicked a Link or Entered Your Password
Immediately call the Security Hotline at extension 4400 (available 24/7), then change your password through the self-service identity portal. Do not wait until the next business day. Early reporting is never punished; delayed reporting increases the damage.

## Malware or Unusual Device Behaviour
Disconnect the device from Wi-Fi and the VPN but do not power it off, because memory evidence may be needed. Call the Security Hotline at extension 4400.

## Lost or Stolen Devices
Report lost or stolen laptops and phones to the IT Service Desk and the Security Hotline within 1 hour of noticing. Include the asset tag if known. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device.

## Response Targets
The SOC acknowledges hotline reports within 15 minutes, 24 hours a day. Confirmed incidents affecting customer or employee personal data are escalated to Legal and the Data Protection Officer.
