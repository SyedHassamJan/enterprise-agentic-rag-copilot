# NovaRetail HR: Manager Guide to Performance Management

Owner: HR Business Partners | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Status: CURRENT | Access: managers

## Before Starting a PIP
Before placing an employee on a Performance Improvement Plan, managers must document at least 2 prior coaching conversations from the last 90 days, consult their HR Business Partner, and use the PIP template from the HR portal. The HR Business Partner must review the draft PIP before it is shared with the employee.

## Running the PIP
Hold a check-in every 2 weeks and record the outcome in the HR portal the same day. Measurable goals must not change during the PIP unless the HR Business Partner agrees.

## Terminations
Managers must never communicate a termination decision before it is approved by both HR and Legal. Termination meetings are held with an HR representative present.

## Sensitive Conversations
Hold performance conversations in a private room or a private video call, never in open office areas or team channels.
