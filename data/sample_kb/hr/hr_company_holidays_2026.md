# NovaRetail HR: Company Holidays 2026

Owner: HR Operations | Applies to: corporate office and remote employees in the United States

## Paid Company Holidays
NovaRetail observes 10 paid company holidays in 2026:
1. New Year's Day: Thursday, January 1
2. Martin Luther King Jr. Day: Monday, January 19
3. Memorial Day: Monday, May 25
4. Juneteenth: Friday, June 19
5. Independence Day (observed): Friday, July 3
6. Labor Day: Monday, September 7
7. Thanksgiving Day: Thursday, November 26
8. Day after Thanksgiving: Friday, November 27
9. Christmas Eve: Thursday, December 24
10. Christmas Day: Friday, December 25

## Floating Holiday
Each employee also receives 1 floating holiday per year that can be used for a personal, cultural, or religious observance. It must be requested in the HR portal like annual leave and does not carry over.

## Store Employees
Store employees follow the retail holiday schedule published by Store Operations, because stores remain open on most company holidays. Store employees who work on a company holiday receive holiday pay at 1.5 times their regular rate.

## Holidays During Leave
A company holiday that falls during approved annual leave is not deducted from the employee's annual leave balance.
