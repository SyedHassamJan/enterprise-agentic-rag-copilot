# NovaRetail HR and Finance: Expense and Travel Policy

Owner: HR and Finance | Effective: 2026-01-01

## Submitting Expenses
Business expenses must be submitted in the NovaSpend expense portal within 30 days of the expense date. Itemised receipts are required for every expense over $25. Expenses submitted after 90 days are not reimbursed except with Finance Director approval. Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll after manager approval.

## Travel
Book all flights and hotels through the company travel portal. Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Business class is allowed for flights of 6 hours or longer with Vice President approval. Hotel rates are capped at $220 per night in standard cities and $320 per night in high-cost cities.

## Meals
The domestic meal per diem is $60 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursable except at approved client entertainment events.

## Home Office Stipend
Employees approved for remote or hybrid work may claim a one-time home office stipend of up to $300 for furniture such as a desk or ergonomic chair. The stipend does not cover IT equipment. Laptops, monitors, docking stations, and other IT equipment are not reimbursable and must be ordered through the IT Hardware Portal.

## Non-Reimbursable Items
Personal mobile phone plans, home internet, traffic fines, and personal entertainment are not reimbursable.
