# NovaRetail HR: Grievance and Whistleblowing Policy

Owner: HR and Legal | Effective: 2026-01-01

## Raising a Grievance
Employees can raise a workplace concern with their manager, their HR Business Partner, or, if the concern involves the manager, with the skip-level manager. Formal grievances are submitted in writing through the HR portal.

## Anonymous Reporting
Concerns about fraud, harassment, discrimination, safety, or legal violations can be reported anonymously through the NovaEthics hotline at 1-800-555-0199 or the NovaEthics web form. The hotline is operated by an independent third party and is available 24/7.

## Investigation Timeline
HR acknowledges every formal grievance within 3 business days. Investigations aim to conclude within 30 days; the employee is informed if more time is needed. Outcomes are shared with the person who raised the concern to the extent permitted by privacy obligations.

## Non-Retaliation
NovaRetail strictly prohibits retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith or participates in an investigation. Retaliation is itself a serious disciplinary offence.

## Appeals
An employee who is not satisfied with the outcome of a grievance may appeal in writing to the Head of HR within 10 business days of receiving the outcome.
