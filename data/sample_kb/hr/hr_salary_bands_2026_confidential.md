# NovaRetail HR: Corporate Salary Bands 2026 (CONFIDENTIAL)

Owner: Total Rewards Team | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Status: CURRENT | Access: hr_staff

## Corporate Salary Bands
- L1 Associate: $48,000 to $62,000
- L2 Specialist: $60,000 to $78,000
- L3 Senior Specialist: $76,000 to $98,000
- L4 Manager: $95,000 to $125,000
- L5 Senior Manager: $120,000 to $155,000

## Offer Approval Rules
Offers up to the band midpoint can be approved by the hiring manager. Offers above the midpoint require HR Business Partner approval. Offers above the band maximum require approval from the Chief People Officer.

## Confidentiality
Salary band data is restricted to the HR team. Do not share band ranges with employees or candidates outside the formal offer process.
