# NovaRetail HR: Employee Benefits Guide 2024 (ARCHIVED)

Owner: Total Rewards Team | Effective: 2024-01-01 | Status: SUPERSEDED by the 2026 Benefits Guide

## Medical Insurance
NovaRetail pays 80% of the employee-only medical premium and 60% of the premium for dependants.

## Retirement Plan (401(k))
NovaRetail matches 50% of employee contributions up to 6% of base salary. Company matching contributions vest over 3 years.

## Wellness Allowance
Every full-time employee receives a wellness allowance of $300 per calendar year for gym memberships.

## Life Insurance and EAP
Life insurance coverage is 1 times base salary. The Employee Assistance Program provides 5 free counselling sessions per year.

## Open Enrollment
Annual open enrollment runs from October 15 to October 31.
