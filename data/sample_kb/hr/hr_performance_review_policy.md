# NovaRetail HR: Performance Review and Promotion Policy

Owner: HR Business Partners | Effective: 2026-01-01

## Review Cycle
NovaRetail runs one annual performance review in December and a mid-year check-in in June. Employees complete a self-assessment, managers write the review, and results are calibrated across teams before being shared. Employees who joined after October 1 are reviewed in the following cycle.

## Ratings
Performance is rated on a 5-point scale: 1 (Does not meet expectations), 2 (Partially meets), 3 (Meets expectations), 4 (Exceeds expectations), 5 (Exceptional). Annual merit increases are based on the December rating.

## Promotions
Promotion nominations are made by managers during the December cycle. Approved promotions take effect on April 1. Off-cycle promotions require approval from both the department director and the HR Business Partner.

## Performance Improvement Plans
An employee rated 1, or rated 2 in two consecutive cycles, may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). A PIP lasts 60 days, defines measurable goals, and includes check-ins every 2 weeks. HR must review every PIP before it starts.

## Disagreeing With a Review
Employees who disagree with their rating may request a review meeting with their skip-level manager and HR Business Partner within 15 business days of receiving the result.
