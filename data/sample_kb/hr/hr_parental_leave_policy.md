# NovaRetail HR: Parental Leave Policy

Owner: HR Policy Team | Effective: 2026-01-01

## Eligibility
Employees become eligible for paid parental leave after 6 months of continuous service. The policy applies equally to birth, adoption, and foster placement, and to parents of any gender.

## Paid Leave Entitlement
- Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
- Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Parental leave may start up to 2 weeks before the expected birth or placement date and must be taken within 12 months of the birth or placement. Secondary caregivers may split their leave into a maximum of 2 blocks.

## Notice
Employees should notify HR and their manager at least 8 weeks before the planned start of leave, or as soon as reasonably possible for adoption or foster placements with short notice. Requests are submitted through the HR portal.

## Return to Work
Employees return to the same or an equivalent role. A phased return of up to 4 weeks at reduced hours (minimum 60%) with full pay is available to primary caregivers on request.

## Interaction With Other Leave
Parental leave is separate from annual leave and sick leave. Annual leave continues to accrue during parental leave.
