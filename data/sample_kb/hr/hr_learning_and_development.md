# NovaRetail HR: Learning and Development Policy

Owner: People Development Team | Effective: 2026-01-01

## Annual Learning Budget
Every full-time employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per calendar year for courses, certifications, conferences, and books related to their current or next role. Unused budget does not roll over to the next year.

## Approval
Learning requests require manager approval before the purchase is made. Requests over $1,000 also require HR Business Partner approval. Approved costs are claimed through NovaSpend with proof of enrollment or completion.

## Learning Days
Employees receive 2 paid learning days per year to attend training or study for certifications. Learning days are separate from annual leave and must be scheduled with the manager.

## Repayment Agreement
If the company pays more than $1,000 for a single course or certification and the employee resigns within 12 months of completion, the employee must repay the cost on a pro-rated basis.

## Mandatory Training
Information security awareness and code of conduct training must be completed within 30 days of joining and every year after that. Overdue mandatory training is reported to the manager.
