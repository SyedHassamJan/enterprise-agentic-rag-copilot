# NovaRetail HR: Employee Benefits Guide 2026

Owner: Total Rewards Team | Effective: 2026-01-01 | Status: CURRENT

This guide replaces the 2024 Benefits Guide.

## Medical Insurance
NovaRetail pays 85% of the employee-only medical premium and 70% of the premium for dependants. Coverage starts on the first day of the month after the start date.

## Retirement Plan (401(k))
NovaRetail matches 100% of employee contributions up to 5% of base salary. Company matching contributions vest immediately. Employees are auto-enrolled at a 3% contribution rate and can change it at any time in the benefits portal.

## Wellness Allowance
Every full-time employee receives a wellness allowance of $500 per calendar year, which can be used for gym memberships, fitness classes, sports equipment, or mental-health apps. Claims are submitted through NovaSpend.

## Life Insurance and EAP
Life insurance coverage is 2 times base salary at no cost to the employee. The Employee Assistance Program (EAP) provides 8 free confidential counselling sessions per issue per year.

## Open Enrollment
Annual open enrollment runs from November 1 to November 15. Changes take effect on January 1. Outside open enrollment, changes require a qualifying life event reported within 30 days.
